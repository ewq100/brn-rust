Harbor handoff study

1. Purpose

This synthetic study compares two inspection routes over six weekday trials.

The observations describe this sample only; weather and staffing were held constant.

2. Findings

Table 1 records median elapsed time and the number of missed handoffs.

Route        Median (min)     Missed handoffs

Check first       12                 1

Test first        19                 3

The check-first route was faster by 7 minutes, but this is not a causal estimate.

Figure 1. Gate order: check, test, then release; each gate must pass before the next.

3. Limits

The trial excluded overnight operations and must not be generalized to storms.

Appendix A. Observation rule

A missed handoff means a parcel waited more than 5 minutes after its release signal.

Observers recorded one timestamp per gate and did not change the route mid-trial.

## AI authority constraint

Follow the owner's [semantic intelligence and deterministic authority](../../../architecture/overview.md#semantic-intelligence-and-deterministic-authority)
clarification. The LLM supplies semantic interpretation, bounded evidence/tool
choices and candidates; Rust/workflow verifies exact evidence and owns approval,
effects and recovery. New task-specific AI behavior uses a small centralized
typed static Rust behavior/prompt boundary within the existing `brn-ai` /
`brn-workflow` architecture, preserving their existing responsibilities. Existing prompts move only when
naturally touched or very small and low risk. No new AI framework or architecture
is authorized; frozen V1 delivery order remains unchanged.

## Outcome and boundaries

The owner can inspect a complete exact review of one disposable original, then
explicitly attest semantic completeness and remove that exact copy recoverably.
Retain catalog, capture proof, approved Source, selected durable outcomes,
rejected dispositions and tentative disagreements. Conversion, AI completion,
proposal application and finding closure never infer completeness. Open conflicts
may remain Open after explicit acknowledgement. No permanent erasure, batch
removal, AI removal tool, vault deletion or additional database.

## Sequential deliverables

1. Complete bounded original-review manifest and shared preview: all related
   processing, Source proposals, analyses, grouped/manual consequences, approval
   journals and findings with exact versions/proofs. One consistent Store read;
   no pagination or clipping. Expose original availability and semantic review
   as still required. This read grants no removal readiness and does not qualify
   current saved Source files; physical Source qualification belongs to the
   separate confirmation gate. First deliverable is this manifest/read contract
   through shared AppWorker and CLI, with membership/version/whole-bound tests.
2. Exact versioned owner attestation and recoverable original removal. Store
   intent and an ordinary immutable receipt precede exclusive rename; retain the
   original at an operation-owned private endpoint. Startup must recognize
   certified removal before capture recovery, avoid resurrection, and preserve
   exact review history through older/fresh database restoration. Qualify current
   durable Actions/completions, proposal rewrite ownership and Undo lineage in
   addition to retained review work; Source freshness/identity stays exact. Replay precedes
   fresh-state qualification and never removes a re-created path.
3. Thin shared AppWorker/CLI/native review and confirmation flows; complete
   scenarios and qualified merged evidence. Integrate one coherent deliverable
   per PR; only acceptance of a concrete result authorizes its namespace effect.

## Acceptance and verification

Synthetic fixtures establish complete ordered membership including late/manual
work, rejected records, newer reviews, failed/interrupted analyses, closed/Open
findings, and original creation digests. Malformed records, stale/ambiguous saved
identity, changed proof/set/version, oversized complete payload and unknown
artifacts refuse safely. All original bytes and existing timestamps survive
refusals. Crash witnesses cover durable intent, rename, sync, receipt and Store
acknowledgement; fresh/older database recovery preserves evidence and never
resurrects a certified removed original. Undo restores only the exact retained
copy and refuses a occupied/replaced endpoint.

Each deliverable gets independent read-only review, technical finding validation,
relevant locked/offline Store/workflow tests, applicable native tests, fixtures
and exact-head Mac/shared CI before normal integration. Use pinned Rust1.98.1,
current-user-owned canonical `/private/tmp/brn-mini-synthetic-20261005`, separate
worktree target and disposable synthetic data. No live calls/model downloads or
private-data operations. Native/live/owner qualification remains separate.

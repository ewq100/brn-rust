Keep Codex allowance, purchased balances and BRN live-provider costs distinct.

A completed remote job still needs its intended result, exact tree, checks and

preserve evidence and continue independent work. When nothing useful is eligible,
perform one bounded backlog review and finish rather than manufacturing activity.

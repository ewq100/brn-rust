# BRN Threads product overview

**Selected direction, 10 October 2026.** The [canonical Threads target](../architecture/threads-target.md) owns the complete requirements, reasoning, tradeoffs, and acceptance journeys. This is its product summary, not a separate specification or a claim of implementation.

## Product promise

Give BRN information and a goal. It keeps relevant working knowledge useful, tracks the work, and brings the owner the decisions that require attention. Changes remain understandable and local work recoverable.

## Four owner concepts

| Concept | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Notes | Useful working knowledge, writing, and full imported references. |
| Threads | Questions, decisions, comments, reviews, and ongoing work linked to relevant notes and Actions. |
| Actions | Suggested, Open, Waiting, Done, or Cancelled work with explicit completion evidence. |
| History | Meaningful changes, sources, reasons, actors, decisions, and recovery. |

Home has a Needs you queue, Ask or delegate input, browsable notes, and quiet recent-change visibility. Routine maintenance does not generate a review item for every source or note.

## Autonomy and owner control

The agent maintains ordinary local notes under standing delegation. Whole-note protection preserves wording or decisions where needed. A clear owner instruction can authorize a specific protected edit without another confirmation.

Protection is different from factual endorsement. Owner confirmation belongs to a specific revision; later changes do not inherit it. Source text cannot grant permissions.

## Two intake choices

**Use information** incorporates relevant information into existing or useful new notes, threads, and Actions. A routine email need not become a permanent note.

**Import full note** keeps a selected paper, process description, or other key document as one complete readable note. Preserve substantive wording, sections, steps, exceptions, tables, equations, meaningful figures, and supplied references/appendices. Repeated page furniture may disappear. Full notes start protected; AI may create linked summaries and comments. Uncaptured substantive content produces a visible partial outcome.

Original emails and files stay external. Intentionally retained full-note content and required assets belong to BRN and survive temporary-input cleanup, offline use, and backup.

## Existing outcomes to preserve

Support English and Estonian, answer in the owner's language, and retain rebuildable retrieval suitable for a personal workspace around 5,000 active notes. Preserve explicit provider/account/model and supported reasoning-effort choices without silent fallback. Current instructions outrank saved preferences; inferred or one-off preferences do not become standing policy automatically.

Headless capabilities use the same scoped service. A connected external agent or possession of the owner CLI is not an authorization grant. Credentials are not knowledge or history.

## First usable release and later work

The first release includes one native GPUI workspace, direct writing, threads/comments, ordinary/protected notes, both intake choices for qualified formats, current search, basic Actions, autonomous local maintenance, meaningful history, review, dependable Undo, Markdown export on demand, and complete BRN backup/restore.

DOCX/PPTX/HTML generation is required later. Direct external sending, broad connector automation, multiplayer/sync, live outside editing, arbitrary branch merging, and a plugin marketplace are deferred.

The owner is not using the old application. Build a fresh core/schema without gradual migration or a permanent legacy runtime. See the [build plan](../work/active/threads-rebuild/plan.md).

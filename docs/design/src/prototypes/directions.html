How we buy paint

This process describes how Serna buys paint for client projects, from specification to delivery.

Steps

  1. The project lead confirms the final specification with the client in writing.
  2. Orders under €500 can be placed directly with Värvikoda; larger orders need a manager's Märt's approval.
  3. Order at least 10 working days before painting starts; Värvikoda's lead time is 5–7 days.
  4. On delivery, check batch numbers against the order and photograph the labels. Keep the receipt in the project folder.

Open questions

Who approves orders when Märt is away?

## Contradictions found in earlier material

- The 2026-09 handoff asked for an editorial **serif** reading font; this round
  uses the system sans for chat and prose (D1). Serif remains a possible reading
  preference.
- The Electron studies offered several **terminal theme variants**; the handoff
  rejected user-selectable directions. This design keeps Dark/Light only.
- Older material used *Review queue*, *pending* and *publish*; the current
  vocabulary is *Needs Review* (findings), *Review* (proposals awaiting a
  decision) and *approval*. *Publish* is not used.
